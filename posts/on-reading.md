I don't have a definitive list of behaviour hacks that will change your life and help you read more. Instead, I have some suggestions that seem to work well for me. Perhaps they'll work for you, too.

## Use books as décor

Seeing books around your humble abode as part of the natural environment makes them more inviting. This is especially true if they're books you haven't read before.

For me, I find my brain prods me and says things like: "Hey, see that book there? You haven't even read it and yet it's on display."

Or, "that book I bought as décor looks really nice, maybe I should give it a try."

Or even, "what if someone you're having over asks about that book and you haven't even read it?" 

Fear of guilt or judgment can be a strong motivator.

I'm also intrigued by a nicely designed book cover. I can't resist. Simple, minimal covers always win me over. I avoid bookstores because I know I'll rationalize some sort of purchase based on how well-designed the front of the book is. (I <em>quite often</em> judge a book by its cover.)

Whichever way your brain rationalizes the interest and desire to pick up the book, it's a good thing.

## 4. Read physical books

As I type this suggestion, I can hear the pain anguish from you, dear reader. Physical books are objectively less accessible than digital ones: the text isn't resizable (sans a magnifying glass), there's no way to ease eye strain with dark mode, you need a light on when it gets dark, they're unusable to blind and low vision readers, they often cost more than their digital counterparts, etc. The list goes on.

Yet I still suggest this because I think there's something special about physical books. (That and it's hard to accomplish suggestion #1 or #2 with a digital book.) Don't try to tell me you can have your phone closer to you than TV remotes and game controllers. Our phones are bigger distractions than any other object.

If you're going to attempt reading on a smart device like your phone or a tablet, _please_ enable some way to stay in the reading app and block all other apps and notifications. Mute texts and emails while you read. Apple devices have this notion of [Focus][apple-focus], where you can choose to block certain apps and people from contacting you once you enable that particular Focus.

Like digital books? Make a "Reading" focus.
