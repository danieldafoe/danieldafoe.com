## What is Operator?

[Operator][operator-openai] is OpenAI's latest 

It seems like it's bent on perfecting what [rabbit][rabbit] attempted to do: giving AI a task to do without it knowing exactly how the website works and allowing it to figure it out for you.

I loved the premise (promise?) and design of rabbit so much so that I pre-ordered it. I subsequently cancelled by pre-orer after watching scathing reviews of its performance.


## What Operator could improve

- Software testing?
  - Here comes another heyday for [Cucumber][cucumber]. Now we can write in plain language again <em>without</em> the need for Gherkin. Operator would become the new swap-out for Gherkin. 
  - "Make sure that dialog is keyboard accessible."
  - "Create a new account"
  - "Sign in and check the expected delivery of my most recent order."

- Accessibility of the web?
  - Would it be able to perform tasks <em>for</em> people? Going one step further than that, would it ensure a more accessible web because it relies on semantics to know what exact is a button to click and what's a link? 
  - Creating buttons out of <div> elements isn't going to help. Sure, perhaps it could reason that something with a class of "btn" or "button" might also be a button, but how much will it try before it can't go any further?

- Improve user "unsatisfcation" with atrocious UX patterns across the web
