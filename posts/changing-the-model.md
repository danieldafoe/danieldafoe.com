In semi-recent history, The New York Times decided the Daily Mini crossword puzzle in their [NYT Games app][nyt-games] would be included as part of a paid subscription in their "Games" or "All Access" packages.

This isn't the first time a company has decided to take something that was once free and make it paid.

The business practice is actually quite popular—and quite successful.

What feels shady about to me, however, is this idea that you offer a metaphorical cornocopia of features (games), see which ones are most liked (presumably Daily Mini crossword), then remove that uber popular option and make it part of your paid bundle.

What's particularly worrisome about this to me is that people have potentially already formed a habit or expectation around this feature.
